## Full Description (4,000 characters max)

Meek Meet is a warm, faith-rooted community platform where everyday people gather,
listen, and turn shared concerns into meaningful action.

**Read**
Explore sacred texts across five traditions — the Bible, Quran, Tanakh, Dhammapada,
and Book of Mormon — with daily verses, weekly themes, bookmarks, and highlights.

**Reflect**
Answer thoughtful, community-minded questions designed to surface what really matters
to the people around you.

**Gather**
Join or lead a local circle that meets on the new moon. Share safely, listen deeply,
and discover the patterns that emerge when many voices speak together.

**Be Heard**
Meek Meet uses anonymised, AI-powered synthesis to turn circle conversations into
community insights — and, when ready, into reports for councils, MPs, and decision-makers.

Built for the meek. Because the meek do not shout — they prove.

Features:
• Multi-tradition sacred text reader
• Curated daily verses and weekly themes
• Local circle discovery and creation
• New moon meeting rhythm
• Shepherd verification and safety tools
• Anonymised community insights
• Direct reports to local authorities

## Data Safety Form (Google Play)

**Data collected:**
- Email address (required for account)
- Name (optional profile)
- Approximate location (only when using Circles)
- User-generated content (responses, bookmarks, service times)
- Verification documents (for shepherd applicants only)

**Data shared:**
- No data sold to third parties
- Anonymised, aggregated insights may be displayed publicly
- Authority reports contain only anonymised summaries

**Data usage:**
- App functionality
- Analytics (anonymised)
- Communication (meeting reminders)
- Safety verification (shepherds)

**Security practices:**
- Encryption in transit
- Row Level Security on database
- Verification for community leaders
